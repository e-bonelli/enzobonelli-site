Real-time voice agent for field-service operations

In production, 2026

A phone agent that replaces a legacy IVR workflow: it takes calls from field technicians, gathers what they need, then reasons and negotiates on their behalf — all in real time over a standard phone line, on self-hosted open-weight models so cost stays predictable.

  1. Step 1 of 5 · The call

    A technician picks up the phone.

    A field technician calls on a regular phone line. The agent replaces a legacy IVR workflow and is in production.

    • Voice AI
    • Telephony
    • Production

  2. Step 2 of 5 · Stream in

    Audio streams in as it’s spoken.

    Twilio Media Streams carries the call audio over a WebSocket into a self-hosted boundary. Nothing is recorded first and processed later.

    • Twilio
    • WebSockets
    • Self-hosted

  3. Step 3 of 5 · Understand

    Speech becomes text, turn by turn.

    Whisper transcribes the call while voice-activity detection handles turn-taking, so the agent knows when the caller has finished.

    • Whisper
    • VAD
    • Streaming

  4. Step 4 of 5 · Reason & negotiate

    The agent does the work.

    An LLM agent on a self-hosted open-weight model runs the intake, gathers the details it needs and handles the follow-up conversation on the technician’s behalf.

    • Open-weight LLM
    • Agentic
    • Self-hosted

  5. Step 5 of 5 · Speak back

    The reply streams back to the call.

    Low-latency text-to-speech streams the answer back through the call. It’s streaming end to end, and self-hosting keeps cost predictable.

    • TTS
    • Low latency
    • Streaming

  6. Complete · The full system

    Real time, self-hosted, in production.

    Speech recognition, reasoning and voice all run inside a self-hosted boundary, streaming end to end over a standard phone line — so the agent sounds immediate and the cost stays predictable.
