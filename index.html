01 · About

A builder for the highest-stakes environments

I'm the Director of AI Engineering at Sears Home Services, leading the AI org that's modernizing one of the largest in-home services networks in the country. My background spans economics, financial engineering, and applied machine learning — and over the past four years I've moved from data engineering at Booz Allen into senior architecture and now executive engineering leadership, delivering AI systems into some of the most constrained environments in the federal space and now at enterprise scale.

My work focuses on agentic architectures, retrieval-augmented and graph-augmented generation (RAG / GraphRAG), and the operational glue that makes large language models trustworthy enough to deploy in production: lineage tracking, auditability, and explainable reasoning. I've led teams supporting the United States Space Force, the DoD Chief Digital and AI Office, the F-35 program, and U.S. Army G-38; I architected enterprise AI document-processing and multi-agent systems at Caylent; and I am a DoD AI hackathon winner, taking first place in the User Interface category at Bravo-Opana.

I hold a TS/SCI clearance and am completing a Doctorate of Engineering in AI & Machine Learning at George Washington University, where my thesis focuses on the economic determinants of AI enterprise integration — the conditions under which firms successfully translate AI capability into measurable economic value. I've earned graduate degrees in Economics and Financial Engineering, publish thinking, mentor engineers, and keep my hands on the keyboard.
